How UniForge Works

A dedicated ecosystem designed from the ground up for university students to unite, build, and succeed.

01

Verified Student Registration

Sign up using your official university credentials or student verification. UniForge is strictly gated for students to preserve an honest, collaborative environment.

02

Build Your Live Portfolio

List your tech stack, university, interests, and GitHub repositories. Highlight what roles you excel in — whether Frontend, Backend, AI/ML, Mobile, or UI/UX.

03

Discover Projects & Hackathons

Post an innovative project idea or browse teams forming for hackathons and competitive programming events. Send and receive team invitations seamlessly.

04

Fulfill Tasks & Boost Your Rating

Deliver on your assigned milestones to earn peer ratings and reputation points. Reliable teamwork elevates your rating, unlocking high-tier teams and partner opportunities.
